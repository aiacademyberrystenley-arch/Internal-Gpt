SRM-GPT — Document Management Guide

SRM Institute of Science & Technology · Staff Training Handout

How to upload documents and how the system decides who can see them. Share this with anyone who manages content.

1.  Who can do what

ActionAdminTeacherStaffStudent
Upload documentsYesYesNoNo
Rename / Delete documentsYesNoNoNo
View / downloadAllYesYesOnly what's shared

In short: Admins & Teachers upload. Only Admins rename or delete. Everyone else only views what they're allowed to see.

2.  How to upload a document

Open the Documents page (left menu) and fill the Upload document form:

  1. Title — a clear, recognisable name. e.g. “CSE Sem 6 Time Table”.
  2. Category — a label such as Notice, Time Table, Syllabus, Circular.
  3. Department — choose from the dropdown: All / General = everyone or a specific one Computer Science & EngineeringScience & HumanitiesManagement …
  4. Semester — the semester number, if relevant (optional).
  5. Academic year — e.g. 2025-2026.
  6. Tags — comma-separated keywords to aid search. e.g. exam, timetable, sem6.
  7. Visible to — tap to pick one or more roles:
    EveryoneStudentTeacherStaff
    You can combine them (e.g. Student + Teacher). Admins always see every document.
  8. Choose the file — supported: PDF, Word (.docx), Text (.txt), CSV, up to 20 MB.
  9. Click Upload and index.

3.  What happens after you upload

The system automatically saves the file, reads the text inside it, and makes it searchable. Watch the Status column (click Refresh after a few seconds):

StatusMeaningWhat to do
ProcessingStill being read.Wait a few seconds, then Refresh.
IndexedDone — now usable in chat answers.Nothing. It's live.
FailedCouldn't read the text (e.g. a scanned-image PDF).Re-upload a clearer / text-based file.
Important: a document only powers chat answers once its status is Indexed.

4.  Who can see a document — the two rules

A person can use a document only if both rules pass:

Rule A — Audience (the “Visible to” you picked): the viewer's role must be in the list, or the audience is Everyone. (Admins bypass this and see all.)

Rule B — Department (students only):

  1. Department = All / General → every student can use it.
  2. Department = a specific one (e.g. CSE) → only students of that department.
  3. A student from a different department gets a polite redirect instead of the answer:
“Dear user, I do have this information — but it is department-specific, so kindly contact the concerned HOD / Dean. Thank you for your understanding. Please keep asking your queries; we are pleased to answer you in a more customised manner.”

Teachers, staff and admins are not department-restricted — they see across departments. This is why the same department dropdown is used at signup and at upload, so they match.

5.  The document list & the chat connection

On the Documents page each row shows Title · Department · Visible to · Status · Actions (download / rename / delete). The list itself is filtered — a teacher never even sees admin-only documents.

When anyone asks a question in the chat, the assistant searches only the documents that person is allowed to see (Rules A + B) and answers from them. So tagging correctly is what makes the chatbot give the right answers to the right people.

One-line summary: Admins/teachers upload a file, tag who can see it (Visible to) and which department it's for. The system indexes it. The chatbot then answers each person only from the documents they're allowed to see — and students only get their own department's documents.
